# Comparable expansions

Facts below come only from the cited sources (accessed 2026-11-05). Where a fact was not
found in the sources consulted, it is marked "not found" rather than estimated.

## Vegas Golden Knights (NHL, 2017)

- **Year:** began play 2017-18. Expansion process began June 2015; Bill Foley's group agreed to the fee about 12 months later. Source: [LVSportsBiz](https://lvsportsbiz.com/2018/12/04/golden-knights-will-have-sister-expansion-team-in-division-new-nhl-seattle-franchise-starts-in-2021-22/)
- **Fee:** $500 million. Source: as above.
- **Arena:** T-Mobile Arena, Paradise, NV (see `data/manual/arenas.csv`, source [Wikipedia list of indoor arenas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_indoor_arenas_in_the_United_States)).
- **Early attendance / sellouts:** not found in sources consulted (nhl.com allowed; not verified in time budget).

## Seattle Kraken (NHL, 2021)

- **Year:** officially joined the NHL in 2021 after final payment. Source: [NHL.com](https://nhl.com/news/seattle-officially-joins-nhl-can-sign-free-agents-make-trades-324191506)
- **Fee:** $650 million, split in equal shares among 30 of 31 clubs (Vegas excluded). Source: as above; [Bleacher Report](https://bleacherreport.com/articles/2942154-seattle-kraken-complete-expansion-payments-officially-welcomed-as-nhl-franchise)
- **Arena:** Climate Pledge Arena, Seattle (see `data/manual/arenas.csv`).
- **Early attendance / sellouts:** not found in sources consulted.
